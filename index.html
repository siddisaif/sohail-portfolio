Method

The design pipeline

A piping model is only as good as the information it starts from. This is the sequence I follow from the first engineering input to the fabrication packages.

STAGE 1
Inputs
P&IDs, line lists, plot plans, equipment vendor data sheets and GA drawings set the basis for routing.
STAGE 2
Layout and routing
Equipment is placed for orientation, access and maintenance clearance, then piping is routed through pipe racks and structure.
STAGE 3
Coordination
Inter-discipline review with process, mechanical, civil, structural, instrumentation and electrical teams. Clash detection closes conflicts before they reach site.
STAGE 4
Deliverables
Stress comments, support design, isometrics, BOM and MTO are issued from the model, then closed through client reviews at 30, 60 and 90 percent.
